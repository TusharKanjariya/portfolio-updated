Privacy

Privacy policy.

Last updated 20 August 2026

This policy explains what this portfolio collects, why it is used, and the choices available to you. The short version: analytics is optional, contact details are not sent to analytics, and this site does not sell personal information.

At a glance

  • Analytics stays off until you allow it.
  • Advertising features are disabled.
  • Contact-form content is not stored by this website.
  • You can change your analytics choice at any time.

Information this site handles

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics may process information such as your device and browser type, approximate location, referring page, pages viewed, and interactions with selected portfolio links. This site does not send your name, email address, phone number, enquiry message, or copied contact values to Google Analytics.

Optional Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 with measurement ID G-WX015PXD1L to understand which portfolio content is useful. The Google tag is not loaded until you select Allow analytics. Google Signals and advertising-personalisation signals are disabled.

When analytics is allowed, the site records page views and limited interactions such as contact actions, a valid contact-form handoff, Medium article clicks, and professional-profile clicks. Learn more in Google Analytics' privacy safeguards ↗ and Google's Privacy Policy ↗.

Your analytics choice and browser storage

Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under tk_analytics_consent as either granted or denied. If you allow analytics, Google Analytics may set cookies whose names begin with _ga. If you decline, the Google tag is not loaded and this site attempts to remove those analytics cookies.

You can also clear this site's cookies and local storage through your browser settings.

Contact enquiries

The contact form prepares a message and opens your email application using a mailto: link. The website does not submit that content to a database or retain a copy. Once you send the email, your email provider and the recipient's email provider process it under their own policies. Correspondence may be retained as reasonably needed to respond to your enquiry or manage a professional relationship.

External websites

This portfolio links to services including Medium, LinkedIn, GitHub, Substack, daily.dev, Dribbble, and employer or education websites. Those services operate independently and may collect information under their own privacy policies. Opening an external link does not give this website access to your account on that service.

Retention and your choices

Your analytics preference remains in local storage until you change it or clear site data. Analytics information is retained according to the configuration of this site's Google Analytics property. You may decline or withdraw analytics consent at any time using the preference control above or in the footer.

Questions or requests

For a privacy question or a request concerning information you have shared by email, contact tusharkanjariya2014@gmail.com. This policy may be updated when the site's tools or data practices change; the date at the top will show the latest revision.
