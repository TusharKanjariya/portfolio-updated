# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The portfolio serves hiring managers, technical recruiters, engineering leaders, founders, and prospective clients. These visitors need to quickly assess Tushar Kanjariya’s professional maturity, technical range, delivery ownership, design judgment, remote-working capability, and suitability for a job or project.

## Product Purpose

This website presents Tushar as an experienced full-stack developer and technical writer with 6+ years of professional experience. It exists to attract relevant employment and client opportunities, make professional credibility easy to evaluate, and grow the audience for his Medium writing. Success means qualified visitors understand his seniority and capabilities, review the evidence behind them, and contact him about a job or project.

## Positioning

An experienced full-stack developer and technical writer who combines professional UI/UX judgment with ownership across frontend, backend, data, infrastructure, delivery, and effective remote collaboration.

## Conversion & proof

- The primary action is contacting Tushar about a job or project.
- Secondary actions are reviewing his professional experience and reading or following his Medium writing.
- The line a visitor should remember after ten seconds: “An experienced full-stack developer and technical writer who can own delivery, collaborate with or guide a team, and work effectively in a remote environment.”
- Belief ladder: Tushar has substantial professional experience; he can own full-stack delivery from interface through infrastructure; his design background improves his product judgment; his career history, certifications, and education demonstrate reliability; his technical writing demonstrates communication and knowledge-sharing ability; he is worth contacting for a role or project.
- Proof on hand, in priority order: employment history and role descriptions; certifications and education; published Medium writing as a secondary professional stream and side-income channel.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, and dependable. The experience should balance authority, technical depth, and approachability. Confident typography, spacious composition, controlled color, and subtle buttery-smooth interactions should create a professional impression without visual noise.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy or excessive animation, dense sections, information overload, generic portfolio templates, overly playful or casual styling, and cold or impersonal corporate presentation.

## Design Principles

1. Demonstrate seniority through clarity and execution.
2. Show end-to-end ownership instead of listing technologies alone.
3. Use design judgment as evidence of stronger engineering.
4. Keep motion subtle, smooth, and purposeful.
5. Make professional contact easy while giving technical writing meaningful visibility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across the responsive website, including keyboard accessibility, readable contrast, adaptable text, semantic structure, and reduced-motion support. Treat practical usability across mobile, tablet, laptop, and large desktop screens as the goal rather than relying on checklist compliance alone.
